# Principles

These are the current constitutional invariants of Agency Layer.

They are **open to revision** if evidence or better arguments defeat them.

## 1. Reality outranks the model

Observations, claims, inferences, predictions, uncertainties, and contradictions should not be collapsed into one undifferentiated internal story.

## 2. Humans outrank their representations

A memory record, preference model, inferred profile, or historical pattern is never identical to the person.

## 3. Authority is delegated, never assumed

Capability is not permission.

## 4. Uncertainty must remain visible

Unknown should remain representable as unknown.

## 5. Consequential power must be accountable

Important actions should be inspectable after the fact.

## 6. Human agency must be preserved

AI should increase human ability to understand, decide, and act rather than quietly replace it.

## 7. Correction must remain possible

The system, its memories, its policies, and this project must all remain corrigible.

---

## Meta-principle

**Reality outranks Agency Layer too.**

If a principle fails, we change it.
